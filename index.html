The Opportunity

Turning classroom finance into practical financial thinking.

MBA coursework builds the theoretical foundation — valuation, corporate finance, markets. A dedicated, student-led platform can extend that foundation into hands-on practice, giving students a structured path from theory to real capability.

The Placement Case

Finance interest in the cohort is well ahead of finance placements.

83
Students in the current SME MBA cohort
Many
Have expressed interest in pursuing finance roles
Few
Finance placements landed by previous SME batches, relative to that interest
Most of us are freshers. The gap isn't a lack of interest — it's a lack of applied, market-facing skill-building before placements. FINSEC's workshops, competitions, and simulations are built to close exactly that gap, directly ahead of placement season.
