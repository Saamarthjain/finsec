/06 · capital-league

The Capital League

The flagship: a semester-long simulated investment competition. Every member trades their own paper account through the term; the season closes with the best accounts defended before a panel.

  1. All accounts open at ₹10,00,000.

    On Monday, 21 September 2026, every core account is credited in full — five accounts, fifty lakh on the table, and trading open to every member who joins after.

  2. Not one rupee is real.

    Prices are live, money isn't. No real-money trading anywhere near club funds. Every mistake here is tuition-free.

  3. No reason, no trade.

    Each position is journaled with its investment thesis, and the thinking gets questioned at the table.

  4. Mondays settle arguments.

    Leaderboard, best trades and worst calls — all out in the open, every week.

  5. December, the defence.

    Finalists defend their portfolio before a panel, scored on more than the headline number:
